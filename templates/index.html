The shared problem

Most disaster advice is written for one hazard. Households live with several.

A flood, a hurricane, a wildfire, and an earthquake do not require four different go-bags or four reunion plans. They do require a few decisions that should not be mixed up. Harbor is where you ask what to prepare once, and what changes when the hazard changes.
